    04 / WORKING & MISCONCEPTIONS

    The reason behind
    the result.

    Two fictional extracts demonstrate how an examiner distinguishes a useful method from a conceptual gap.

    QUESTION 05 · CIRCLE THEOREMS2 / 6 MARKS

    A central angle subtending arc AB is 128°. Find the angle at the circumference standing on the same arc.

    ILLUSTRATIVE STUDENT ANSWER

    128°

    Copies the central angle rather than using the angle relationship.

    EXAMINER CORRECTION

    128° ÷ 2 = 64°

    The angle at the centre is twice that at the circumference on the same arc.

    Judgement: Diagram recognition earns limited credit; the key angle theorem is not applied correctly. Teach the relationship visually before introducing another diagram.

    QUESTION 09 · QUADRATIC MODELLING4 / 7 MARKS

    A rectangle has sides x cm and (x + 3) cm and area 40 cm². Find its dimensions.

    x(x + 3) = 40

    WHAT WENT WELL

    Amelia sets up the area equation and obtains the two algebraic candidates x = 5 and x = −8.

    WHAT WAS MISSING

    The negative length must be rejected and both dimensions stated: 5 cm and 8 cm.

    Judgement: The modelling set-up is promising; reasoning must continue through interpretation and a final answer in context.

    Signature principle: The aim is to explain why marks were earned or lost, not to relabel every mistake as a lack of knowledge.

    05 / TARGETED RECOVERY PLAN

    Make the next
    four weeks count.

    A focused cycle that repairs misconceptions and collects new evidence. Targets describe tasks and observable methods—not guaranteed mark increases.

    01
    Week 1 · Circle-theorem reasoning

    Draw and label three centre/circumference angle diagrams. Explain the 2:1 relationship in words, then solve four unseen items. Check whether a theorem is stated before calculating.

    02
    Week 2 · Read statistical displays accurately

    Revisit the median and quartile positions on a cumulative-frequency graph. Annotate axes and locate the upper quartile before attempting three new interpretations.

    03
    Week 3 · Complete modelling answers

    Solve two fresh quadratic modelling problems. Identify candidate solutions, reject invalid values in context, check dimensions and state the final answer with units.

    04
    Week 4 · Reassess transfer

    Complete a short unseen mixed review covering all three targets. Compare the method shown with today's annotations before making a further teaching decision.

    Success evidence to look for

    A correct theorem with explanation; correct quartile location; a complete contextual conclusion in a quadratic model.

    If the same error returns

    Reduce the complexity, model one worked example, ask for a verbal explanation and then reassess using a different question.

    Suggested workload should be adapted to the learner and existing teaching. This report supports, rather than replaces, professional judgement.

      07 / A FEW IMPORTANT DETAILS

      Every detail.
      Thoughtfully considered.

      A clear routine, helpful reports and the right support around the learner — without turning mathematics into another source of pressure.

      Can my child's tutor or parent see the dashboard?

      Separate assigned-tutor and authorised read-only parent accounts are included only with Grove Exam. Weekly and Daily provide student-facing reporting but no independent parent or tutor accounts. Access is individually permissioned.

      Are all answers human marked?

      No. Routine marking is digital or structured only where reliable. Complex handwritten reasoning requires appropriate professional assessment. Signature is the separately arranged human-marked product.

      Does a long practice streak produce a predicted grade?

      No. An indicative grade outlook may appear with Grove Exam only when enough valid, qualification-specific examination evidence exists. Otherwise it is intentionally withheld.

      What if a student misses a practice day?

      The aim is a manageable habit, not an unbroken streak. The useful next step is to return to a focused task, repair a misconception and review improvement without unnecessary pressure.

      Are all the listed examination banks ready now?

      No. MathsGrove's exam route directory includes planned coverage. Questions must be authored, independently checked and mapped to the appropriate qualification before deployment. This website shows clearly labelled original specimens.

      Does MathsGrove offer live tutoring?

      No. MathsGrove complements independent study and existing lessons with practice, stored working and assessment insights. A Grove Exam tutor is a professional assigned by the partner organisation.
