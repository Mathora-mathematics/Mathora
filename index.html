      07 / A FEW IMPORTANT DETAILS

      Every detail.
      Thoughtfully considered.

      A clear routine, helpful reports and the right support around the learner — without turning mathematics into another source of pressure.

      Can my child's tutor or parent see the dashboard?

      Separate assigned-tutor and authorised read-only parent accounts are included only with Grove Exam. Weekly and Daily provide student-facing reporting but no independent parent or tutor accounts. Access is individually permissioned.

      Are all answers human marked?

      No. Routine marking is digital or structured only where reliable. Complex handwritten reasoning requires appropriate professional assessment. Signature is the separately arranged human-marked product.

      Does a long practice streak produce a predicted grade?

      No. An indicative grade outlook may appear with Grove Exam only when enough valid, qualification-specific examination evidence exists. Otherwise it is intentionally withheld.

      What if a student misses a practice day?

      The aim is a manageable habit, not an unbroken streak. The useful next step is to return to a focused task, repair a misconception and review improvement without unnecessary pressure.

      Are all the listed examination banks ready now?

      No. MathsGrove's exam route directory includes planned coverage. Questions must be authored, independently checked and mapped to the appropriate qualification before deployment. This website shows clearly labelled original specimens.

      Does MathsGrove offer live tutoring?

      No. MathsGrove complements independent study and existing lessons with practice, stored working and assessment insights. A Grove Exam tutor is a professional assigned by the partner organisation.
