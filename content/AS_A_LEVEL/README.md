# Level 3 · AS and A Level Mathematics (later content phase)
**This is the third academic content area.** It is intentionally not merged with GCSE or IGCSE questions.

## Separate tracks to research before authoring mocks
- Pearson Edexcel UK GCE Mathematics 9MA0 / Further Mathematics 9FM0: A Level specifications, pure/stats/mechanics component mapping.
- Pearson Edexcel International AS/IAL Mathematics: modular unit routes P1–P4, S1, S2, M1 etc. with their current specification and entry codes.
- Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics 9709 and Further Mathematics 9231: papers and variants by syllabus year.
- AQA and OCR UK AS/A Level Mathematics and Further Mathematics: distinct format and optional content.
- IB Mathematics AA/AI and TMUA are **separate** qualification families, not generic AS/A Level papers.

## Original item standards
Proof, functions/inverses/composites, rational algebra, graphs and transformations, binomial, trig identities/equations, differentiation/integration, series, probability distributions, mechanics modelling and clear assumptions. Include clean numerical answers, justified domain restrictions and mark-scheme alternative approaches.
