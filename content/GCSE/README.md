# Level 1 · GCSE Mathematics (England)
**Relevant awarding bodies:** AQA (8300), Pearson Edexcel (1MA1), OCR (J560), Eduqas; Wales WJEC qualification and transition require an independent specification version.

## Six syllabus domains
1. Number: indices, standard form, operations, bounds and estimations.
2. Algebra: expressions, equations, functions, inequalities, sequences and graphs.
3. Ratio, proportion and rates of change: percentages, gradients, compound measures and modelling.
4. Geometry and measures: shape, circle theorems, transformations, trigonometry, bearings, area/volume, 3D.
5. Probability: sample spaces, conditional reasoning and combined events.
6. Statistics: sampling, averages, scatter graphs, distributions and interpretation.

## Quality and demand
- Foundation: fluent procedures and accessible situations, progressing to multistep reasoning.
- Higher: algebraic fluency, proof, functional reasoning, modelling and unfamiliar contexts.
- Every full paper targets appropriate AO1, AO2 and AO3 coverage. AQA outlines Foundation 50/25/25 and Higher 40/30/30; check each board's assessment guidance before paper assembly.
- Original 1–2 mark quick starts; 3–4 mark method questions; 5–6+ mark extended reasoning and modelling, with clear M/A/B mark schemes.
- Diagrams must be accurate where measurements are implied, or explicitly state 'not drawn accurately'. Always label axes, units, angles and figure conditions.
- Calculator allowance is a **paper rule**, not inferred from the presence of a difficult expression.

## Official paper templates
- AQA 8300 / Pearson Edexcel 1MA1: three 90-minute, 80-mark papers; paper 1 non-calculator.
- OCR J560: three 90-minute, 100-mark papers per tier; paper 02/05 non-calculator.
- Eduqas: two 135-minute, 120-mark components (non-calculator/calculator).
- WJEC Wales: 2025 syllabus reform means new specification checks before mock configuration.

## Planned production batches
A. 20 well-moderated Foundation questions each in Number, Algebra, Ratio, Geometry, Probability, Statistics.
B. 20 Higher questions each in the six domains, including AO2/AO3.
C. Original paper packs A and B for each verified board/tier/paper component **only when their sums and content coverage match official targets**.
D. Moderated worked methods, alternatives, dependent method marks, possible follow-through and diagnostic misconceptions stored **privately**.
