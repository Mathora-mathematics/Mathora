# MathsGrove — GCSE & IGCSE exam-board research (28 October 2026)

This matrix is based on **official awarding-body subject/specification information**, not copied question papers. Paper formats are research data; original mock sets still require question writing and moderation. Verify the published specification for the **actual examination year**, especially across syllabus reforms.

## Question-writing principles derived from the research

- A **question family** is classified by its skill, syllabus, tier, mathematical demand and calculator allowance; multiple different numerical versions still count as the same family for paper-overlap checks.
- The same mathematical technique can appear in different contexts, but its accepted style, calculator conditions, language, mark scheme and numerical accuracy may differ by board.
- Each original item must have a **fully independently checked solution** and examiner-only marking guidance. Mark schemes need proper M/A/B/FT conventions where suitable, not an automatically guessed '1 mark per line'.
- Each graded paper should include controlled fluency, reasoning and application; a random draw from a tiny bank cannot reproduce board structure or a meaningful grade prediction.
- An exam-timed mock requires **a database enforced deadline** and a frozen, moderated question list, not just a browser countdown.
- The current public website already supports typed working, squared/lined digital whiteboards, pens/colours/highlighters, and 3 per-question photo uploads. These are the evidence types to preserve in timed mode.

## Production sequence

**Phase 1 (this revision):** research; board directory; taxonomy; 68 *unpublished* A/B paper blueprints; 24 original *unapproved* sample stems; private staff bank inspection and optional authoring/moderation.

**Phase 2:** author and check complete GCSE and IGCSE question families; actual full-mark A/B papers; private original diagrams; second-examiner quality control; separate board-year publication.

**Phase 3:** server-backed timed sitting, strict expiry and locked uploads, extra-time accommodations, resumption, audit logs, automated/manual marking distinction and real reports.

**Phase 4:** AS/A Level board-by-board research and paper bank; then IB/TMUA separate programme families.

The labelled blueprints are a **production queue, not 68 mock papers already available to students**.
