# Level 2 · International GCSE Mathematics
**Separate pathways, not a single 'IGCSE' paper format:**

- Cambridge Mathematics 0580 and Mathematics (9–1) 0980 for **2025–2027**: Core P1 non-calculator and P3 calculator (90 minutes, 80 marks); Extended P2 non-calculator and P4 calculator (120 minutes, 100 marks). Cambridge 0980 grading and regional eligibility differ from 0580.
- Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A (linear 4MA1): Papers 1/2 at Foundation or Higher, 120 minutes / 100 marks each; calculator permitted.
- Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Mathematics A modular (4XMA1): separate Unit 1 and Unit 2, per tier, 120 minutes / 100 marks; **only international centres**. Unit-specific content needs separate mapping.
- OxfordAQA 9260: Core Paper 1C/2C, 90 minutes / 80 marks; Extension 1E/2E, 120 minutes / 100 marks; calculator permitted.
- Pearson Mathematics B, Cambridge Additional Mathematics 0606 and Cambridge International Mathematics 0607 are **distinct qualifications**: do not silently map them to 0580.

## Topic domains and marking
Number; Algebra & graphs; coordinate geometry; geometry and mensuration; trigonometry; transformations and vectors; probability; statistics. Questions progress from accessible computation to structured multistep work, interpretation and mathematical communication.

The Cambridge AO2 requires students to **analyse, interpret and communicate mathematically**, not just calculate. Method (M), accuracy (A), independent (B), and follow-through (FT) are understood according to the relevant board/mark scheme; don't blindly assume the same conventions across boards.

## Content writing batches
A. Cambridge 0580 Core P1/P3 original bank with calculator flags.
B. Cambridge 0580 Extended P2/P4 original bank and exact non-calculator surds, algebra and trigonometry.
C. Pearson Edexcel 4MA1 Higher/Foundation, with integrated calculator work and modelling.
D. Cambridge 0980 and OxfordAQA board-specific validation/wording.
E. Paper A/B variants assembled only after sufficient original, approved questions have been authored.

**Avoid the Cambridge 2024 trap:** older pre-2025 0580 papers use different calculator and component formats. All mocks must carry a syllabus year.
