# Question and mock quality gates
1. **Originality & copyright:** write independent content, avoid copying board-owned paper wording, mark schemes or diagrams. Cite specifications for alignment, not endorsement.
2. **Maths correctness:** independent complete solution; double-check signs, domains, extraneous roots, probability sample space, units, exactness/rounding and numerical values.
3. **Syllabus fit:** current subject code and *exam year*, Core/Extended or Foundation/Higher, calculator conditions, AO and question demand. A superficially similar topic may not belong to each route.
4. **Mark scheme:** credit method and final accuracy with clearly distinguishable M/A/B, accepted equivalents, dependency, special cases, alternate approaches, partial credit and suitable misconception feedback. Marks sum to the stated total.
5. **Diagrams:** source an original vector diagram from structured data; labelled measurements, scales, vertices, 3D perspective, bearings and axes checked. No misleading drawing without 'not to scale'.
6. **Accessibility:** plain English, precise quantities, readable mobile formatting, MathML/accessible labels, keyboard drawing alternative (typed and photo upload).
7. **Paper assembly:** correct marks/timing, distribution of topics and cognitive demand, no duplicate structures between mock variants, review end-to-end by second examiner.
8. **Security:** staff-only answer key, tenant-scoped drafts, editorially approved questions and separate board-specific publishing gate, no publicly discoverable unseen question PDFs, RLS verified.
9. **Timed exam:** server-locked deadline, honest upload/expiry behavior, accommodations, audit log. Never claim real exam invigilation equivalence.
10. **Release:** keep private draft and reviewed status; publish to learner only after examiner or documented automated marking rule validation. Grade prediction requires board/year-standardized evidence, not invented boundaries.

A bank of ten pilot questions cannot supply credible full papers. Blueprints are planning records, not finished papers.
