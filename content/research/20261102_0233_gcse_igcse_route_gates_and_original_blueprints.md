## Board differences and examination-year gates
- AQA 8300 and Pearson UK 1MA1: three papers, each 90min/80 marks; P1 noncalculator, P2/P3 calculator; same tier and exam series. UK GCSE Foundation AO1/AO2/AO3 50/25/25, Higher 40/30/30. Ofqual decision of 5 May 2026 requires formula sheets for the remaining lifetime of current specifications, including resits. Never expire access automatically in 2027.
- Cambridge 0580: Core P1 noncalculator and P3 calculator (90min/80 marks each); Extended P2 noncalculator and P4 calculator (120min/100 each). Core AO1 60–70%, AO2 30–40%; Extended AO1 40–50%, AO2 50–60%. The official 2028–30 syllabus is **Version 2** via the Cambridge hub; a direct URL web cache can display obsolete Version 1. Exam year determines syllabus. Core C1.8 standard-form calculations only P3, C1.10 no calculated-result bounds, C8.3 combined events only with replacement. E2.12 differentiation Extended only.
- Pearson International linear 4MA1: 1F/2F or 1H/2H each 120min/100 marks, calculators permitted; Higher includes elementary differentiation, unlike UK GCSE. Do not import UK 1MA1 Paper 1 noncalculator rule.
- Pearson International modular 4WM: 4WM1F/H and 4WM2F/H are distinct units, each 120min/100 raw marks and up to 120 UMS. Unit 2 assumes Unit 1. Pearson specifies **no mixing Foundation and Higher across the two units**. Higher Unit 2 §3.4 calculus and §2.8 quadratic inequalities are not Unit 1 topics. Raw % is not UMS; series-specific conversions needed.

## 30 original blueprint designs
**D:** targeted micro-skill 2–3 minutes; **W:** multi-step weekly 6–9 minutes; **E:** reasoning-rich Exam-tier practice 9–14 minutes, NOT a full timed mock. Mark allocations are provisional; examiner must produce a complete rubric.

### 1. AQA GCSE 8300 Foundation
**Objectives:** A2/A5 linear equations, S4 averages, R9 and A9 linear cost models.
**Gate:** P1 noncalc; P2/P3 calc; UK formula sheet by exam year.
**Likely misconceptions:** Mean versus median; fixed fee versus variable fee.
- **Grove Daily:** Daily: Solve 3(2x−1)=21; verify. Answer x=4, 3(8−1)=21. [2m,2min]
- **Grove Weekly:** Weekly: Scores 8,10,12,14 and unknown fifth have mean 12. Find fifth, median and range; explain why mean need not be an observation. Total 60, missing 16, median 12, range 8. [6m,7min]
- **Grove Exam practice:** Exam practice: Hire plans A=£4+£1.80d and B=£7+£1.20d for d hours. Determine break-even, both costs at d=8, and cheaper plan for d>5. d=5; £18.40 vs £16.60; B cheaper. Explain assumption of constant per-hour rates. [8m,10min]
**Dedup fingerprint:** linear-tariff/quiz-missing-mean.

### 2. AQA GCSE 8300 Higher
**Objectives:** A7 inverse function, N8 surd arithmetic, A22 quadratic inequalities and modelling.
**Gate:** P1 noncalc possible; no differentiation in UK GCSE.
**Likely misconceptions:** Inverse versus reciprocal; conjugate; strict versus inclusive interval.
- **Grove Daily:** Daily: If f(x)=4x+7, find f⁻¹(x) and check f(f⁻¹(9))=9. Answer (x−7)/4, inverse input 9 maps to 1/2. [3m,3min]
- **Grove Weekly:** Weekly: Rationalise (√3+1)/(√3−1) and verify by multiplication. Answer 2+√3. [5m,7min]
- **Grove Exam practice:** Exam practice: Model R(x)=x(18−x) pounds for integer tickets x, 1≤x≤18. Find x with R≥65, then maximum R. (x−5)(x−13)≤0, integers 5..13; maximum £81 at x=9. State integer/domain assumptions. [9m,12min]
**Dedup fingerprint:** integer-ticket-revenue/conjugate.

### 3. Pearson UK GCSE 1MA1 Foundation
**Objectives:** A25 linear sequences, N2 clock arithmetic, G16 compound areas.
**Gate:** Three 90min/80 papers; P1 noncalc; P2/3 calc; same tier/series.
**Likely misconceptions:** nth term vs term number; base-60 minutes; internal edge vs exterior boundary.
- **Grove Daily:** Daily: Sequence 7,11,15,19,... find nth and 20th term. 4n+3; 83. [2m,2min]
- **Grove Weekly:** Weekly: Train leg 08:42–10:15, wait 10:15–10:27, bus leg 10:27–11:05. Find travel, wait and total elapsed. 93,38,12 and 143 minutes. [6m,7min]
- **Grove Exam practice:** Exam practice: Attach a semicircle of diameter 8m to the short side of a 14m×8m rectangle. Find combined area and exterior perimeter exactly. 112+8π m² and 36+4π m; shared diameter excluded from outer boundary. Diagram must show external attachment. [8m,10min]
**Dedup fingerprint:** semicircle-attached-rectangle/clock.

### 4. Pearson UK GCSE 1MA1 Higher
**Objectives:** A4 algebraic fractions, A9 perpendicular lines, A18 simultaneous quadratic/linear equations.
**Gate:** UK GCSE, not 4MA1; no calculus; P1 noncalc.
**Likely misconceptions:** cancel factors not terms; perpendicular slope negative reciprocal; both coordinates needed.
- **Grove Daily:** Daily: Simplify (x²−9)/(x²+5x+6), with excluded inputs. (x−3)/(x+2), x≠−3,−2. [3m,3min]
- **Grove Weekly:** Weekly: P(2,1),Q(8,5). Find midpoint, slope PQ, perpendicular bisector equation and y-intercept. (5,3), 2/3, y=−3x/2+21/2, intercept 21/2. [7m,9min]
- **Grove Exam practice:** Exam practice: Intersect y=x²−6x+5 with y=2x−7. x²−8x+12=0; points (2,−3),(6,5); latter above x-axis; separation 4√5. Explain why x-only answers are incomplete. [9m,12min]
**Dedup fingerprint:** perpendicular-bisector/parabola-line.

### 5. Cambridge IGCSE 0580 Core (2025–27 or 2028–30 v2)
**Objectives:** C1.2 two-set Venn, C1.11 ratio, C1.12 average speed.
**Gate:** P1 noncalc 90min/80, P3 calc 90min/80; C1.8 arithmetic only P3; C1.10 input bounds only; C8.3 replacement only.
**Likely misconceptions:** overlap double count; total time versus moving stages; replacement distinction.
- **Grove Daily:** Daily: In 24 pupils, n(French)=14, n(Spanish)=11, both=6. How many neither? Union 19, neither 5. [3m,3min]
- **Grove Weekly:** Weekly: Divide 96 tokens 3:4:5, verify total and find first share as percentage. 24,32,40 and 25%. [5m,6min]
- **Grove Exam practice:** Exam practice (P3 calculator): A cyclist travels 4km at 12km/h, 6km at 18km/h, 2km at 8km/h without stops. Total time 1/3+1/3+1/4=11/12h=55min, distance 12km, mean speed 144/11≈13.09km/h. Explain why arithmetic mean of speeds is wrong. [9m,12min]
**Dedup fingerprint:** three-speed-overall-mean/two-set-Venn.

### 6. Cambridge IGCSE 0580 Extended (2025–27 or 2028–30 v2)
**Objectives:** E2.12 polynomial differentiation; E1.10 bounds of calculated results; extrema on closed intervals.
**Gate:** P2 noncalc 120min/100, P4 calc 120min/100; not Core; specify exam year.
**Likely misconceptions:** derivative vs function; strict upper bound; endpoint extrema.
- **Grove Daily:** Daily: f(x)=5x²−4x+1. Find f′(x) and gradient at x=3. 10x−4; 26. [3m,3min]
- **Grove Weekly:** Weekly (P4): Panel 6.2cm×4.0cm, both to nearest 0.1cm. Bound area. 6.15≤L<6.25; 3.95≤W<4.05; 24.2925≤A<25.3125 cm². [7m,9min]
- **Grove Exam practice:** Exam practice: On 0≤x≤4, f=x³−6x²+9x+4. f′=3(x−1)(x−3). Local max (1,8), min (3,4). Endpoints (0,4),(4,8). Global max 8 at x=1,4; global min 4 at x=0,3. Require derivative sign reasoning and endpoint check. [10m,14min]
**Dedup fingerprint:** cubic-endpoint-tied-extrema/area-bounds.

### 7. Pearson International GCSE 4MA1 linear Foundation
**Objectives:** §4.9 circle measures, §4.8 right-triangle trig, §4.9 area and unit coverage.
**Gate:** Linear 1F+2F both 120min/100 calculator, not UK 1MA1 or modular.
**Likely misconceptions:** radius vs diameter; tan ratio; doorway not painted; whole-tin optimization.
- **Grove Daily:** Daily: Circle radius 6cm: exact area and circumference. 36π cm²; 12π cm. [3m,3min]
- **Grove Weekly:** Weekly: A 5m ladder has foot 3m from wall. Height 4m, angle arctan(4/3)≈53.13°. At same angle, a 7.5m ladder reaches 6m. [6m,8min]
- **Grove Exam practice:** Exam practice: Wall 6m×3m minus 2m×1m door; coverage 4m²/L. Tins 1L £5, 2.5L £12, 5L £23. Paintable 16m²; need 4L; cheapest 4×1L=£20 vs £22,£23,£24 alternatives. [8m,11min]
**Dedup fingerprint:** paint-tin-integer-min-cost/ladder.

### 8. Pearson International GCSE 4MA1 linear Higher
**Objectives:** §3.4 elementary differentiation, algebraic fractions, §3.3 reciprocal graph.
**Gate:** Linear 1H+2H calculators; calculus allowed; not UK GCSE or modular Unit 1.
**Likely misconceptions:** differentiate constant; cancellation domain; reciprocal asymptote never attained.
- **Grove Daily:** Daily: f(x)=2x²−3x+5. f′(x)=4x−3, f′(2)=5. [3m,3min]
- **Grove Weekly:** Weekly: (x²−4)/(x²+x−6) simplifies to (x+2)/(x+3), x≠2,−3; evaluate at x=0 gives 2/3. Require factorised steps. [6m,8min]
- **Grove Exam practice:** Exam practice: y=1/(x−2)+3. Asymptotes x=2,y=3; intersections y=5 at (5/2,5), y=2 at (1,2); y=3 impossible since reciprocal term cannot be zero. [8m,11min]
**Dedup fingerprint:** translated-reciprocal-asymptotes/domain.

### 9. Pearson International GCSE modular 4WM2F/01 Foundation Unit 2
**Objectives:** §1.6F reverse percent, §2.6 simultaneous linear, §4.10 cylinder volume and litre conversion.
**Gate:** Unit 2 120min/100 raw; calculator/formula sheet; Unit 1 prerequisite; no tier mixing.
**Likely misconceptions:** reverse multiplier; solving equations; πr²h; 1000cm³=1L.
- **Grove Daily:** Daily: Sale price £48 after 20% reduction; original £48/0.8=£60. [2m,2min]
- **Grove Weekly:** Weekly: Adult ticket a, child c: a+c=15, 2a+3c=35. Solve a=10,c=5 and verify both. [6m,8min]
- **Grove Exam practice:** Exam practice: Six cylinders radius 5cm height 20cm filled from 10L tank. One 500π cm³=π/2 L, six 3π L≈9.42478L; tank sufficient, remainder 10−3π≈0.575L. [8m,11min]
**Dedup fingerprint:** six-cylinder-volume/ticket-equations.

### 10. Pearson International GCSE modular 4WM2H/01 Higher Unit 2
**Objectives:** §2.8 quadratic inequalities, §3.4 calculus in kinematics, §2.7D simultaneous linear/quadratic.
**Gate:** Unit 2 120min/100 raw, 120 UMS maximum; calculator; Unit 1 prerequisites; no Unit 2 calculus on Unit 1.
**Likely misconceptions:** strict endpoints; velocity sign vs distance; two intersections.
- **Grove Daily:** Daily: Solve (x−2)(x−7)<0: 2<x<7 (open endpoints). [3m,3min]
- **Grove Weekly:** Weekly: s=t³−6t²+9t for 0≤t≤4. v=3(t−1)(t−3), rest t=1,3; s at t=0,1,3,4 is 0,4,0,4. Net displacement 4m; total distance 12m. [8m,11min]
- **Grove Exam practice:** Exam practice: Line y=x+1 intersects circle x²+y²=13. Solve x²+x−6=0: (2,3),(−3,−2); distance 5√2. Check both satisfy circle and line. [9m,12min]
**Dedup fingerprint:** signed-velocity-total-distance/line-circle.
