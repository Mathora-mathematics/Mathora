## Board-specific routing findings (important corrections)
1. **9709 P4 is not Pearson mechanics.** Cambridge P4 §4.2 is **one-dimensional** motion; §4.3 conservation of momentum is **one-dimensional direct impact**, including coalescence; **impulse and coefficient of restitution are not required**. Cambridge explicitly says **vector notation will not be used in its P4 question papers**. Pearson UK 9MA0 §§7.3–7.5 includes 2D vector kinematics/projectiles; Pearson IAL M1 §2 uses i,j vectors, §4.3 includes impulse, and M2 §4.2 includes restitution. Never send an IAL M2 restitution item to 9709 P4. [C9709 pp.31–33] [P9MA0 pp.36–38] [PIAL pp.44–49].
2. **Gravity conventions differ.** Cambridge 9709 P4 says g≈**10 m/s²** is expected in mainly numerical questions; Pearson UK 9MA0 default **9.8 m/s²**, although a problem can specify 10. Always set `gravity_m_s2` at item level, not by global default. [C9709 p.33] [P9MA0 §8.3].
3. **9709 P5 has geometric distribution and E(X), Var(X) of discrete variables; UK 9MA0 does not have the same E/Var calculation requirement.** Cambridge P5 §5.4 includes Geo(p) supported on positive integers; UK 9MA0 Statistics §4.1 explicitly **excludes calculation of discrete random-variable means and variances**. Pearson IAL S1 §5.3 explicitly **includes** E(X), Var(X). Avoid generic "A Level Statistics" filters. [C9709 p.35] [P9MA0 p.33] [PIAL pp.55–56].
4. **9709 P6 does not require explicit CDF knowledge, while Pearson IAL S2 does.** Cambridge P6 §6.3 expects direct density integrals for probabilities and percentiles but states explicit CDF knowledge is excluded; Pearson IAL S2 §§2.2–2.3 explicitly teaches F(x) and f(x)=F'(x). Use separate `cdf_explicit` objective gate. [C9709 p.38] [PIAL p.58].
5. **Inferential scope differs.** Cambridge P6 §§6.4–6.5 includes informal CLT, confidence intervals for mean and large-sample proportion, and Type I/II error probabilities; Pearson UK 9MA0 §5.3 tests normal mean with known/assumed variance and explicitly does **not** require CLT or large-sample approximations. Pearson IAL S1 has no significance testing for PMCC; S2 §4.6 includes binomial-p and Poisson-mean tests. [C9709 pp.38–39] [P9MA0 p.35] [PIAL pp.56,59].
6. **Pearson IAL is modular.** WST01 and WME01 are IAS units; WST02 and WME02 have advanced prerequisites and may only be assigned if the learner is taking that unit. Units are 90 minutes/75 marks with calculators and formulae booklet. UK 9MA0/03 is **one** 120-minute/100-mark paper with both statistics and mechanics. Cambridge P4/P5/P6 are each 75 minutes/50 marks. [C9709 pp.14–16] [P9MA0 pp.5–6] [PIAL pp.8–11,44–59].
7. **Question style:** Cambridge 9709 paper questions are compulsory, often multipart with method working; Pearson IAL official S1 sample contains seven questions totalling 75 marks, but seven is a *sample*, not a mandatory fixed count. Cambridge announced accessibility-layout changes from March 2026 without changing demand. Build responsive working space and full method-mark evidence, not just MCQ answer selection. [C9709 official past-paper hub] [PIALsamples].

### Independent seed sanity checks (not publication approval)
- 9709 P4 energy: initial KE = ½·2·12² = 144 J; uphill force = 2·10·sin30°+3 = 13 N; stopping distance 144/13 m. Check whether a separate friction coefficient is *absent*: 3 N is stated constant resistance, so do **not** add μR.
- 9709 P5 upper 10% threshold: 500 + 8·1.28155 ≈ 510.25 g. Normal approximation for B(100,0.1) uses μ=10, σ²=9, and P(Y≥15) approximated with Y>14.5, not 15.
- 9709 P6 PDF: ∫0³ kx dx = 9k/2 = 1 gives k=2/9. Median m satisfies m²/9=1/2, hence m=3/√2.
- 9MA0 mechanics connected particles: downhill driving force = 2·9.8−3·9.8·sin30°=4.9 N; total mass 5 kg, a=0.98 m/s²; T=2(9.8−0.98)=17.64 N.
- IAL M1 two-stage pulley: a=(3−2)·9.8/5=1.96 m/s²; v²=2·1.96·1=3.92; extra ascent after impact = 3.92/(2·9.8)=0.20 m.
- IAL M2 restitution: 2·5=2v1+v2 and v2−v1=0.5·5=2.5, giving v1=2.5, v2=5; KE before 25 J, after 18.75 J, loss 6.25 J. Lamina centroid: (24(3,2)−4(5,3))/20=(2.6,1.8).
