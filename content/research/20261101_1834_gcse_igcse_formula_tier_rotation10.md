## Findings and exact learning-objective gates
1. **NEW: UK GCSE formulae sheets after 2027.** Ofqual's **5 May 2026** decision extends maths formula-sheet provision **for the remaining lifetime of current UK GCSE specifications, including resits**, not only to 2027. Each series's sheet must be published by 1 September in the preceding year and supplied clean in the exam. Questions cannot be answerable solely by copying from the sheet. This policy is **not automatically applicable to IGCSE**.
2. **AQA 8300 / Pearson UK 1MA1:** Foundation and Higher each sit three 90-minute/80-mark papers, P1 non-calculator and P2/P3 calculator. All papers same tier. UK GCSE subject content is common; AQA S3 histogram, A15 gradient/area under curves, A16 circle tangent and P9 conditional probability are Higher-only. Pearson 1MA1 teaching guidance S3 explicitly assesses frequency density for unequal intervals. Neither UK GCSE assesses polynomial differentiation.
3. **Cambridge 0580 2025–27 v3 / 2028–30 v2:** Core P1 noncalculator 90min/80 marks + P3 calculator 90min/80; Extended P2 noncalculator 120min/100 + P4 calculator 120min/100. Each component 50%. Official Cambridge FAQ confirms a **page-2 formula sheet from 2025 onwards**, while unlisted formulae still require recall.
4. **Exact Cambridge gates:** Core **C1.8** conversion into/out of standard form is allowed on noncalculator P1, but **calculating with values in standard form is expected only on calculator P3**. Core **C1.10** is bounds on rounded input values, **not bounds of calculated results**; Extended **E1.10** includes result bounds. Core **C8.3** combined events only **with replacement**; Extended **E8.3** with/without replacement and **E8.4** conditional probability; symbolic P(A|B) notation/formula is not required.
5. **Pearson International GCSE 4MA1 linear:** two 120-minute/100-mark papers, both calculator-permitted, own tier formulae sheets. Foundation/Higher are **not** interchangeable labels with Cambridge Core/Extended. Higher 4MA1 includes elementary calculus and 3D trigonometry; UK 1MA1 does not.
6. **Pearson 4WM modular:** Unit 1 and Unit 2 each have Foundation/Higher paper (4WM1F/H, 4WM2F/H), each 120 min/100 raw marks, calculator and formula sheet. Unit 2 assumes Unit 1 prerequisites, but Unit 1 must not assess Unit 2-only functions/differentiation. 120 UMS per unit, 240 UMS cash-in: **do not equate raw marks with UMS or predict grades without series conversion**.
7. **Official publicly accessible examples and specimen papers:** examined for skill scope, diagram conventions and method-mark style only. **No source stems, data, figures, solutions or mark schemes copied**. Short MCQs cannot be labelled authentic full multi-mark papers.

### 1. AQA GCSE 8300 Foundation
**Objectives:** N15 / S2 / R13. **Paper gate:** P1 noncalc, P2–P3 calc; 2027 formula sheet.
**D:** Round 7.386 kg to 2 d.p. and write its possible true interval. **Checked result:** 7.39 kg; [7.385,7.395) kg. **Misconception:** Upper bound excluded..

**W:** A 120-person survey records 54 train, 42 bus and 24 walking. Find fractions and pie-chart angles; verify total. **Checked result:** 162°,126°,72°; total 360°. **Misconception:** Treating percentages as degrees..

**E:** Three identical pumps fill 180 L in 4 min. At the same rate, how many litres can five pumps deliver in 9 min? Explain constant-rate assumption. **Checked result:** 15 L/min per pump; 675 L. **Misconception:** Scaling only time, not pump count..

### 2. AQA GCSE 8300 Higher
**Objectives:** A20 / S3 / A15. **Paper gate:** A15 and S3 Higher only; 2027 UK sheet.
**D:** Iterate x_(n+1)=sqrt(7+x_n), x_0=3; find x_1,x_2 to 3 d.p. **Checked result:** 3.162, 3.188. **Misconception:** Using previous rounded value too early..

**W:** Histogram bins [0,5),[5,15),[15,25) with frequencies 10,30,20. Find density, modal class and grouped median estimate. **Checked result:** Densities 2,3,2; modal [5,15); median≈11.67. **Misconception:** Frequency vs density; estimate vs exact..

**E:** Velocity–time vertices (0,0),(4,8),(10,8),(14,0) with straight edges. Find initial acceleration, distance and mean speed. **Checked result:** 2 m/s²; 80 m; 40/7 m/s. **Misconception:** Area versus gradient..

### 3. Pearson UK GCSE 1MA1 Foundation
**Objectives:** N13 / G13 / S2–S4. **Paper gate:** P1 noncalc, P2–P3 calc; UK GCSE sheet.
**D:** Convert 0.006 m³ of water to litres and justify the cubic conversion. **Checked result:** 6 L. **Misconception:** 1 m³=1000 L, not 100 L..

**W:** A cuboid is 5×3×2 cm. Give front elevation (width 5), plan dimensions and volume. **Checked result:** Front 5×2, plan 5×3; 30 cm³. **Misconception:** Mixing plan/elevation..

**E:** 20 travellers: bus 8, walk 7, cycle 5. Find pie-chart angles, projected counts for 120, and a sampling limitation. **Checked result:** 144°,126°,90°; 48,42,30. **Misconception:** Assuming sample necessarily representative..

### 4. Pearson UK GCSE 1MA1 Higher
**Objectives:** S3 / R16 / A16. **Paper gate:** Higher only; no polynomial differentiation.
**D:** Histogram bins widths 3,8 have frequencies 12,16. Calculate densities. **Checked result:** 4 and 2. **Misconception:** Histogram height is density..

**W:** P_0=200, P_(n+1)=0.85P_n+12. Find first three updates and fixed point. **Checked result:** 182,166.7,153.695; fixed point 80. **Misconception:** Dropping the +12 in recurrence..

**E:** For x²+y²=25 and P(3,4), prove P is on circle, derive tangent and x-axis intercept. **Checked result:** 3x+4y=25; intercept 25/3. **Misconception:** Tangent gradient must be negative reciprocal..

### 5. Cambridge IGCSE 0580 Core
**Objectives:** C1.10 / C1.8 / C1.11–C1.12. **Paper gate:** P1 90min/80 noncalc; P3 90min/80 calc; sheet on p2.
**D:** A measurement is 5.6 cm to nearest 0.1 cm. Give complete possible interval. **Checked result:** 5.55≤L<5.65 cm. **Misconception:** Do not ask for bounds of a calculated result at Core..

**W:** Paper 1: convert 0.00042 to standard form and 6.7×10^5 to ordinary form; compare magnitudes. **Checked result:** 4.2×10^−4; 670000; second larger. **Misconception:** C1.8 arithmetic with standard form belongs on P3..

**E:** Rice recipe: 4 cups for 6 people. Serve 15 people using sealed 3-cup packs. How many packs and cups left? **Checked result:** 10 cups required; 4 packs; 2 cups left. **Misconception:** Rounding packs down..

### 6. Cambridge IGCSE 0580 Extended
**Objectives:** E8.4 / E9.3 / E8.3–E8.4. **Paper gate:** P2 120min/100 noncalc; P4 120min/100 calc.
**D:** A group has 20 red tickets, 8 winners. Among red tickets what fraction wins? **Checked result:** 2/5. **Misconception:** Condition on red subgroup; P(A|B) notation not required..

**W:** Grouped intervals [0,10),[10,20),[20,30) frequencies 4,10,6. Estimate mean using midpoints. **Checked result:** 16 (estimate). **Misconception:** Not exact individual mean..

**E:** 4 blue, 6 red counters. Draw two without replacement. Find P(one of each), P(second blue given first red), P(first blue given one of each). **Checked result:** 8/15, 4/9, 1/2. **Misconception:** Changing denominators and conditional sample space..

### 7. Pearson International GCSE 4MA1 Foundation
**Objectives:** 4.9 A / 6.2 / 4.8 A–C. **Paper gate:** Linear 1F+2F; 120min/100 marks each; calculators.
**D:** Convert 0.025 m² to cm². **Checked result:** 250 cm². **Misconception:** Square unit conversion..

**W:** Score 2 frequency 3, score 5 frequency 4, score 8 frequency 3. Find mean and median. **Checked result:** Total 10; mean=5, median=5. **Misconception:** Weighted frequency..

**E:** Boat sails 6 km east, then 8 km north. Find route length, straight-line displacement and 3-digit bearing. **Checked result:** 14 km,10 km,037°. **Misconception:** Bearing clockwise from north..

### 8. Pearson International GCSE 4MA1 Higher
**Objectives:** 6.2 C / 4.11 / 4.8 D–F. **Paper gate:** Linear 1H+2H; calculators; Higher 3D trig.
**D:** For 2,4,5,8,9,12,13,15 find IQR using median-of-halves convention. **Checked result:** Q1=4.5,Q3=12.5,IQR=8. **Misconception:** State quartile convention..

**W:** Similar solids length ratio 3:5; smaller volume 81 cm³. Find larger volume. **Checked result:** 375 cm³. **Misconception:** Cube length scale factor..

**E:** Cuboid 3×4×12 cm. Find floor diagonal, space diagonal and angle between line and base plane. **Checked result:** 5 cm,13 cm,arcsin(12/13)≈67.38°. **Misconception:** Angle to plane uses floor projection..

### 9. Pearson modular 4WM1F
**Objectives:** §3.3 G / §2.7 A / §6.1–6.3. **Paper gate:** Unit 1 Foundation; 120min/100 raw; calculator.
**D:** Find gradient joining (−2,3) and (4,15). **Checked result:** 2. **Misconception:** Consistent coordinate subtraction..

**W:** Two consecutive positive integers multiply to 156. Form and solve monic quadratic; select admissible root. **Checked result:** n²+n−156=0; n=12 (reject −13). **Misconception:** Negative root context..

**E:** Train early14 late6; walk early9 late11. Find total, P(early), P(train or late), explain overlap. **Checked result:** 40,23/40,31/40. **Misconception:** Double-counting intersection..

### 10. Pearson modular 4WM1H
**Objectives:** §1.4 / §6.3 / §4.8 C–F. **Paper gate:** Unit 1 Higher; no Unit 2 functions/calculus.
**D:** Rationalise 3/(sqrt(5)+1). **Checked result:** 3(sqrt(5)−1)/4. **Misconception:** Use conjugate..

**W:** Bag has 5 green,7 yellow. Without replacement find P(two green), P(second green given first yellow). **Checked result:** 5/33,5/11. **Misconception:** Second draw denominator 11..

**E:** Triangle sides 7 and 9 cm enclose 60°. Find third side and area with correct formulae. **Checked result:** sqrt(67) cm; 63sqrt(3)/4 cm². **Misconception:** Cosine rule vs sine area formula..

### 11. Pearson modular 4WM2F
**Objectives:** §5.2 / §4.11. **Paper gate:** Unit 2 Foundation; Unit 1 prerequisites allowed.
**D:** Reflect P(−4,2) in y=x. **Checked result:** (2,−4). **Misconception:** Swap coordinates, not negate..

**W:** Scale plan 1:400 rectangle 3×2 cm. Find real side lengths and area. **Checked result:** 12×8 m; 96 m². **Misconception:** Area scale squared..

**E:** Reflect triangle (0,0),(2,0),(0,3) in y-axis, then translate by (4,−1). Find image and compare area. **Checked result:** (4,−1),(2,−1),(4,2); area 3. **Misconception:** Transformation order and signs..

### 12. Pearson modular 4WM2H
**Objectives:** §4.6 C(iv) / §5.1 / §3.2 B–D. **Paper gate:** Unit 2 Higher; functions and circle theorems.
**D:** Cyclic quadrilateral opposite angle to 73°. Find angle and theorem. **Checked result:** 107°. **Misconception:** Opposite angles supplementary..

**W:** u=(3,−2),v=(−1,5). Find 2u−v and magnitude. **Checked result:** (7,−9); sqrt(130). **Misconception:** Vector component subtraction..

**E:** f(x)=sqrt(x+4), x≥−4; g(x)=2x+1. Domain of f(g(x)), solve f(g(x))=5 and invert f. **Checked result:** x≥−5/2; x=10; f^−1(y)=y²−4, y≥0. **Misconception:** Domain restriction and false ± square-root branch..
