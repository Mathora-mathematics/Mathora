## Exact objectives, board differences and exclusion gates
- Cambridge 9709 2026–27 current syllabus is Version 4 (December 2025); the change was an updated weblink. 2028–30 uses Version 1.
- Cambridge P2 is AS-only and 2.5 explicitly excludes general substitution; P3 includes separable DEs; P4 excludes impulse/restitution; P5 permits chart drawing; P6 has Poisson tests and Type I/II.
- Pearson UK 9MA0 Pure /01,/02 synoptic 2h/100m; /03 Statistics AND Mechanics 2h/100m. Familiarity with large data set is expected, but public examples here use original data.
- Pearson IAL P1–P4, S1, M1 each 90min/75m. S1 chart drawing/median calculations not direct exam focus; outlier rule must be specified. M1 moments and impulse differ from Cambridge P4.

### C9709-P1 — Cambridge 9709 P1, 1.6 Series
**Eligibility:** P1 AS/A; positive integer binomial only. **Misconception:** n vs n−1 in u_n; inequality reversal for log r<0.
**C9709-P1-D · Grove Daily · 3 proposed marks · 180 s:** GP starts at 18, ratio 2/3. Find term 4.

*QA checkpoint:* 16/3.
**C9709-P1-W · Grove Weekly · 7 proposed marks · 540 s:** Monthly gifts start at £240 and shrink by 10% monthly. Find first-six total and first month when cumulative gifts exceed £2000.

*QA checkpoint:* S6=£1124.5416; S_n>2000 first at n=18.
**C9709-P1-E · Grove Exam · 8 proposed marks · 660 s:** Positive GP has sum∞ 75, first-two sum 27, and 0<r<1. Find a,r and least n with S_n≥95% of S∞.

*QA checkpoint:* r=4/5,a=15,n=14.

### C9709-P2 — Cambridge 9709 P2, 2.5 Integration
**Eligibility:** AS-only P2; no general substitution; MF19. **Misconception:** strip width; half-angle; concavity.
**C9709-P2-D · Grove Daily · 3 proposed marks · 180 s:** Evaluate ∫[0,π/4] cos(2x) dx.

*QA checkpoint:* 1/2.
**C9709-P2-W · Grove Weekly · 7 proposed marks · 540 s:** For y=cos²x on [0,π/3], estimate area with two trapezia, find exact area, and identify error direction.

*QA checkpoint:* T=11π/48≈0.719948; exact=π/6+√3/8≈0.740105; under.
**C9709-P2-E · Grove Exam · 9 proposed marks · 720 s:** For y=1+cos(2x) on [0,π/4], estimate area with two trapezia, integrate exactly and explain under/over by y''.

*QA checkpoint:* T=π(5+√2)/16≈1.259428; exact=π/4+1/2≈1.285398; y''≤0 so under.

### C9709-P3 — Cambridge 9709 P3, 3.8 Separable DE
**Eligibility:** A-Level P3; P1 prerequisites; no integrating factor. **Misconception:** integration constant; sign; equilibria.
**C9709-P3-D · Grove Daily · 3 proposed marks · 210 s:** dy/dt=−0.3y, y(0)=80. Find y(t).

*QA checkpoint:* 80e^(−0.3t).
**C9709-P3-W · Grove Weekly · 7 proposed marks · 540 s:** dy/dt=k(100−y), y(0)=20, y(5)=60. Find k and y(10).

*QA checkpoint:* k=ln2/5; y(10)=80.
**C9709-P3-E · Grove Exam · 10 proposed marks · 780 s:** dy/dt=ky(1−y/120), y(0)=30, y(4)=60. Find y(t), y(8), first t when y=100, and equilibria.

*QA checkpoint:* y=120/(1+3e^(−kt)), k=ln3/4; y8=90; t=4ln15/ln3≈9.8599; equilibria 0,120.

### C9709-P4 — Cambridge 9709 P4, 4.3 Momentum
**Eligibility:** 1D direct impact; impulse and restitution NOT required. **Misconception:** signed velocities; KE not generally conserved.
**C9709-P4-D · Grove Daily · 4 proposed marks · 240 s:** 2 kg cart at +5 m/s collides with 3 kg at −1 m/s; they stick. Find velocity.

*QA checkpoint:* +1.4 m/s.
**C9709-P4-W · Grove Weekly · 7 proposed marks · 540 s:** 4 kg at +3 m/s and 2 kg at −2 m/s stick. Find velocity and KE lost.

*QA checkpoint:* v=4/3 m/s; loss=50/3 J.
**C9709-P4-E · Grove Exam · 9 proposed marks · 720 s:** 2 kg at +6 m/s and 3 kg at −2 m/s collide. Afterwards 2 kg moves at −1 m/s. Find other velocity, KE loss, and explain energy change.

*QA checkpoint:* v=8/3 m/s; KE initial 42 J, final 35/3 J; loss=91/3 J.

### C9709-P5 — Cambridge 9709 P5, 5.2 Combinations and 5.3 Probability
**Eligibility:** Linear restrictions allowed; no circular arrangements. **Misconception:** ordered vs unordered; gap counting.
**C9709-P5-D · Grove Daily · 3 proposed marks · 180 s:** Choose three from five women and four men with exactly two women. Count selections.

*QA checkpoint:* C(5,2)C(4,1)=40.
**C9709-P5-W · Grove Weekly · 7 proposed marks · 540 s:** From six researchers and four analysts select four with at least two analysts. Count and find probability.

*QA checkpoint:* 115 favourable /210 total=23/42.
**C9709-P5-E · Grove Exam · 8 proposed marks · 660 s:** Arrange five distinct textbooks and three distinct novels in a row. Count arrangements with no adjacent novels and with at least one adjacent pair.

*QA checkpoint:* 5!C(6,3)3!=14400; complement 8!−14400=25920.

### C9709-P6 — Cambridge 9709 P6, 6.1 Poisson and 6.5 Tests
**Eligibility:** P5 prerequisite; Type I/II; correct one-sided tail. **Misconception:** p-value vs point probability; Type I vs II.
**C9709-P6-D · Grove Daily · 4 proposed marks · 240 s:** X~Poisson(2). Find P(X≥3).

*QA checkpoint:* 1−5e^(−2)≈0.323324.
**C9709-P6-W · Grove Weekly · 7 proposed marks · 540 s:** H0:λ=2 vs H1:λ>2, X~Poisson(λ), observed X=6. Test at 5%.

*QA checkpoint:* P(X≥6|λ=2)≈0.0165636; reject H0.
**C9709-P6-E · Grove Exam · 9 proposed marks · 720 s:** Test H0:λ=3 vs H1:λ<3 at 5% for one Poisson count. Find rejection region, actual Type I probability and Type II probability if λ=2.

*QA checkpoint:* Reject X=0; Type I=e^(−3)≈0.049787; Type II=1−e^(−2)≈0.864665.

### P9MA0-PURE — Pearson UK 9MA0/01–02, rational binomial
**Eligibility:** Both Pure papers synoptic; 2h/100 marks; not IAL. **Misconception:** negative-index coefficients; convergence domain.
**P9MA0-PURE-D · Grove Daily · 4 proposed marks · 240 s:** Expand (1+2x)^(−1) through x²; give validity.

*QA checkpoint:* 1−2x+4x², |x|<1/2.
**P9MA0-PURE-W · Grove Weekly · 7 proposed marks · 540 s:** Expand (1−3x)^(−1/2) through x² and estimate 1/√0.94.

*QA checkpoint:* 1+(3/2)x+(27/8)x²; at x=.02 gives 1.03135; |x|<1/3.
**P9MA0-PURE-E · Grove Exam · 9 proposed marks · 720 s:** Expand (2+x)^(−1) through x³, estimate 1/2.1, calculate absolute error and justify convergence.

*QA checkpoint:* 0.5−x/4+x²/8−x³/16; x=.1 gives .4761875; exact 10/21; error≈2.97619×10^(−6); |x|<2.

### P9MA0-STAT — Pearson UK 9MA0/03, sampling/binomial tests
**Eligibility:** Paper 3 has Statistics AND Mechanics; familiar large-data-set context, original data only. **Misconception:** sampling bias; independence; wrong tail.
**P9MA0-STAT-D · Grove Daily · 3 proposed marks · 210 s:** Systematic sample of 10 from 80 numbered records with random start 3: list records.

*QA checkpoint:* 3,11,19,27,35,43,51,59,67,75.
**P9MA0-STAT-W · Grove Weekly · 7 proposed marks · 540 s:** 20 independent checks pass with probability 0.2. Find P(at least 7 passes), and state two model assumptions.

*QA checkpoint:* Bin(20,.2) upper tail≈0.0866925; independent, fixed p.
**P9MA0-STAT-E · Grove Exam · 9 proposed marks · 720 s:** A new method is claimed to raise p above 0.30. Seven of 12 independent trials succeed. Test at 5% and discuss biased trial selection.

*QA checkpoint:* H0:p=.3,H1:p>.3; P(X≥7)≈.03860084; reject, but inference depends on representative trials.

### PIAL-P3 — Pearson IAL WMA13/01 P3, functions/differentiation
**Eligibility:** 90min/75 marks; P1–P2 prerequisites; not P4. **Misconception:** ln sum law; chain factor; maximum check.
**PIAL-P3-D · Grove Daily · 3 proposed marks · 180 s:** Differentiate y=xe^(2x).

*QA checkpoint:* e^(2x)(1+2x).
**PIAL-P3-W · Grove Weekly · 6 proposed marks · 480 s:** Solve ln(x+1)+ln(x−1)=ln8 over reals and justify domain.

*QA checkpoint:* x>1; x²−1=8 ⇒ x=3.
**PIAL-P3-E · Grove Exam · 8 proposed marks · 660 s:** For x≥0, f(x)=e^(−x)(x²+2x). Find and classify its stationary point and maximum output.

*QA checkpoint:* f'=e^(−x)(2−x²); x=√2 maximum; value≈1.173871.

### PIAL-P4 — Pearson IAL WMA14/01 P4, 3D vector lines
**Eligibility:** 90min/75 marks; P1–P3 prerequisites; scalar product allowed. **Misconception:** ignoring z in intersection; acute vs obtuse angle.
**PIAL-P4-D · Grove Daily · 3 proposed marks · 180 s:** For r=(1,0,2)+t(2,1,−1), find point at t=3.

*QA checkpoint:* (7,3,−1).
**PIAL-P4-W · Grove Weekly · 7 proposed marks · 540 s:** Do lines r=(1,2,0)+s(1,0,1) and r=(3,1,2)+t(−1,1,0) intersect? Check all coordinates.

*QA checkpoint:* y→t=1, x→s=1, z gives 1≠2; skew.
**PIAL-P4-E · Grove Exam · 8 proposed marks · 660 s:** Lines r=(1,0,1)+s(1,2,0) and r=(3,4,1)+t(0,−1,2) meet. Find intersection and acute angle.

*QA checkpoint:* (3,4,1), s=2,t=0; cosθ=2/5; θ≈66.4218°.

### PIAL-S1 — Pearson IAL WST01/01 S1, 2.1–2.4 data comparison
**Eligibility:** Drawing charts and raw median/IQR calculation not direct examination focus; outlier rule specified. **Misconception:** quartile convention; outlier not automatically error.
**PIAL-S1-D · Grove Daily · 3 proposed marks · 240 s:** For ordered 2,4,6,8,10,12,14,16,18, find median and IQR using lower/upper-four medians.

*QA checkpoint:* median=10,Q1=5,Q3=15,IQR=10.
**PIAL-S1-W · Grove Weekly · 7 proposed marks · 540 s:** Compare A: 11,14,17,21,23,29 and B: 12,13,19,22,27,28 using a back-to-back stem plot. Compare medians and ranges.

*QA checkpoint:* A median19 range18; B median20.5 range16.
**PIAL-S1-E · Grove Exam · 9 proposed marks · 720 s:** A: 12,15,16,18,20,22,24,25,40; B: 13,16,18,19,20,21,22,23,27. Compare median/IQR; identify outliers using 1.5×IQR fences.

*QA checkpoint:* both medians20; A IQR9, upper fence38→40 outlier; B IQR5.5, upper fence30.75→none.

### PIAL-M1 — Pearson IAL WME01/01 M1, 6.1 Moments
**Eligibility:** 90min/75 marks; g=9.8 specified; unlike Cambridge P4, Pearson M1 also covers impulse. **Misconception:** weight at midpoint; force and moment balances.
**PIAL-M1-D · Grove Daily · 3 proposed marks · 180 s:** Perpendicular 4 N force acts 0.30 m from pivot. Find moment.

*QA checkpoint:* 1.2 N m.
**PIAL-M1-W · Grove Weekly · 7 proposed marks · 540 s:** Uniform 4 m horizontal beam mass 6 kg, vertical supports at both ends, extra 2 kg at x=1 m. g=9.8. Find reactions.

*QA checkpoint:* Rright=34.3 N, Rleft=44.1 N.
**PIAL-M1-E · Grove Exam · 9 proposed marks · 720 s:** Uniform 5 m beam mass 4 kg supported at ends, downward P at x=3 m, left reaction 35 N. g=9.8. Find P and right reaction.

*QA checkpoint:* Rright=P+4.2; 5Rright=98+3P ⇒ P=38.5 N, Rright=42.7 N.
