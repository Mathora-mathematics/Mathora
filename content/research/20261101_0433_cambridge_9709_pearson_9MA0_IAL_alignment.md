**High-impact differences:**
- **Discrete variance:** Cambridge 9709 P5 §5.4 and Pearson IAL S1 §5.3 **do** include E(X), Var(X); Pearson UK 9MA0 Paper 3 Statistics §4.1 **explicitly excludes calculation of mean/variance of discrete random variables**. A generic 'A_LEVEL statistics' bank must not route such tasks to 9MA0 as if they were 9MA0 core assessment.
- **Geometric vs binomial:** Cambridge 9709 P5 §5.4 includes **Geo(p)** and B(n,p); Pearson IAL S1 covers discrete variables and normal, with **binomial introduced in S2**, not S1. Pearson 9MA0 Paper 3 §4.1 includes binomial and discrete uniform but does not require geometric-distribution formulae.
- **CLT/confidence intervals:** Cambridge 9709 P6 §6.4 explicitly includes an informal CLT, unbiased variance and confidence intervals; Pearson 9MA0 Statistics §5.3 explicitly says **knowledge of CLT is not required**. These must not be silently shared.
- **Mechanics:** Cambridge 9709 P4 §4.3 covers direct-impact momentum but *not* impulse or coefficient of restitution; its §4.1/§4.4 uses friction and particle models and generally **g=10**. Pearson 9MA0 includes 2D vector kinematics §7.3–7.5 and moments §9.1; its default **g=9.8** unless specified. Pearson IAL **M1** includes impulse/momentum §4.3, moments §6.1 and friction §4.4/§5.3; projectiles/variable-acceleration vector calculus are **M2** content, not M1. 
- **Paper styles:** 9709 P4 assumes particle model and **does not use vector notation in question papers**, while 9MA0 mechanics explicitly uses i/j vector form. Do not automatically reuse identical notation or diagram conventions.

## 2. Exact objective map and misconception targets

| Board / component | Verified official objective | Misconception to diagnose |
|---|---|---|
| Cambridge 9709 P1 **§1.4** | Circular measure: radians, arc length and sector area; select appropriate geometry. | Using degrees directly in s=rθ or A=½r²θ. |
| Cambridge 9709 P1 **§1.6** | Arithmetic/geometric series, sums and application of formulae. | Confusing term index with number of terms; ratio sign and convergence assumptions. |
| Cambridge 9709 P1 **§1.8** | Definite integration; bounded area, area between curves, volumes of revolution. | Treating signed integral as always equal to geometric area. |
| Cambridge 9709 P4 **§4.1** | Resolve forces; equilibrium; friction F≤μR versus limiting F=μR. | Automatically setting F=μR in non-limiting static equilibrium; friction direction. |
| Cambridge 9709 P4 **§4.2/4.3** | Displacement/velocity graphs; one-dimensional conservation of momentum, including coalescence. | Distance vs signed displacement; assuming kinetic energy conserved in a sticking collision. |
| Cambridge 9709 P5 **§5.2/5.4** | Permutations with repetition/restrictions, **not circular arrangements**; binomial/geometric random variables and E/Var. | Treating indistinguishable objects as distinct; Geo(p) off-by-one support r≥1. |
| Cambridge 9709 P6 **§6.2/6.4** | Linear combinations (independence required for variance addition), sample mean variance σ²/n, informal CLT and confidence intervals. | Using n instead of √n for SE; summing SDs rather than variances; forgetting independence. |
| Pearson UK 9MA0 Pure **§§1.1, 7, 8, 9** | Proof; differentiation/integration; numerical methods and their limitations. | Claiming numerical iteration proves uniqueness; ignoring interval and validity conditions. |
| Pearson UK 9MA0 Stats **§4.1, §4.3, §5.2** | Binomial modelling; critique distribution assumptions; binomial hypothesis test and interpretation. | Equating failure to reject H0 with proof H0; one-tailed vs two-tailed; unsupported discrete Var(X) calculations. |
| Pearson UK 9MA0 Mechanics **§7.3/7.5, §8.6, §9.1** | Vector SUVAT/projectiles, friction, moments. | Resolving vertical gravity in wrong direction; moments about inconsistent pivot; F=μR for every static case. |
| Pearson IAL P1 **P1.3 topics coordinate geometry/trigonometry/differentiation** | Straight lines/circles, trigonometric methods, derivatives and integration within P1. | Applying reciprocal slope when lines are parallel, confusing gradient of normal/tangent. Verify micro-topic number at item authoring. |
| Pearson IAL S1 **§2.3, §4.1–4.3, §5.2–5.3** | Dispersion; least-squares regression and interpolation; discrete CDF and E/Var. | Confusing F(x) with P(X=x); regression of x on y swapped; extrapolation overclaimed. |
| Pearson IAL M1 **§4.2–4.4, §5.3, §6.1** | Connected-particle dynamics, impulse/momentum, friction, moments. | Equal/opposite forces applied to same body; forgetting direction in impulse; friction inequality in equilibrium. |
