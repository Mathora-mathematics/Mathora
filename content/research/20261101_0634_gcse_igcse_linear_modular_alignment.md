# MathsGrove — GCSE/IGCSE official alignment, 1 November 2026

**Rotation:** AQA 8300, Pearson UK 1MA1, Cambridge 0580 (2025–27), Pearson International GCSE Mathematics A linear 4MA1 and modular 4WM. Prior research covered Cambridge/Pearson A Level, Further Mathematics, IB AA/AI and TMUA. **Status:** research-only editorial blueprints; no new student questions or timed mocks published.

## 1. Important qualification and assessment differences

| Route | Authentic assessment | Critical content gate |
|---|---|---|
| AQA GCSE 8300 | Foundation/Higher; three 90-minute 80-mark papers: P1 non-calculator, P2 and P3 calculator | Foundation content versus Higher-only A7 inverse/composite, G10 circle theorems, P9 conditional |
| Pearson UK GCSE 1MA1 | Same three-paper 90-minute/80-mark calculator structure as AQA, but Pearson's own papers | UK GCSE does **not** assess differentiation |
| Cambridge IGCSE 0580 Core (2025–27 v3) | P1 90 min/80 non-calculator; P3 90 min/80 calculator, 50% each | C7.1 single transformations only; C8.3 combined probability with replacement only; no C8.4 conditional |
| Cambridge IGCSE 0580 Extended | P2 120 min/100 non-calculator; P4 120 min/100 calculator, 50% each | E2.12 polynomial differentiation, E7.4 vector geometry, E8.3 with/without replacement, E8.4 conditional |
| Pearson International GCSE Mathematics A **linear 4MA1** | 1F/2F or 1H/2H; two 120-minute, 100-mark **calculator-permitted** papers | Higher 3.4 A–E calculus; Foundation 4.8 right-angle trig; not UK 1MA1 |
| Pearson International GCSE Mathematics A **modular 4WM** (first examined 2025) | Unit 1 F/H 4WM1F/4WM1H; Unit 2 F/H 4WM2F/4WM2H. Each 120 minutes, 100 marks, calculator permitted, 50%; cash-in **4XMAF** or **4XMAH** | Separate unit objectives and prerequisites. Only available to schools outside UK; do not substitute 4MA1 papers |

**New finding: Pearson 4WM modular must become a separate MathsGrove route.** The Pearson 2024 modular specification (Issue 2, March 2024) splits the same overall Mathematics A content across **different units**. Unit 1 covers basic number/accuracy, simple quadratics, trig and basic probability, plus Higher-only surds, completing the square, sine/cosine rules and conditional probability. Unit 2 covers ratios, percentage change, simultaneous equations, sequences, volume and similarity, plus Higher-only functions, differentiation, circle theorems and vectors. For example, **4WM1H** has 6.3 C conditional probability but **4WM2H** has 3.2 D inverse/composite functions and 3.4 B–E calculus. Knowledge from Unit 1 is assumed for Unit 2; that does not make Unit 2-only content valid in a Unit 1 mock. Modular unit codes, tier and exam year must be explicit in question and student records. Official [modular specification](https://qualifications.pearson.com/content/dam/pdf/International%20GCSE/Mathematics%20A/2024/specification-and-sample-assessments/int-gcse-mathematics-spec-a-modular.pdf), pp. 6–8, 13–40, 41–44, 53; [qualification page](https://qualifications.pearson.com/en/qualifications/edexcel-international-gcses/mathematics-a-2024-modular.html).

**Second finding: 2027 UK GCSE formulae sheets.** AQA confirmed on **1 September 2026** that tier-specific formulae sheets will accompany **every** GCSE Maths paper in 2027, unchanged in content from 2026; Ofqual's policy applies to UK GCSE mathematics, including Pearson. Non-calculator does **not** mean no formula sheet. Cambridge 0580 has its own per-paper formula lists; Pearson IGCSE has its own supplied formulae. Include formula-sheet version in the mock renderer. [AQA announcement](https://www.aqa.org.uk/news/gcse-maths-sciences-formulae-equation-sheets-2027-28); [Ofqual decision](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/proposed-changes-to-the-assessment-of-mathematics-physics-and-combined-science-gcses-in-2025-2026-and-2027/outcome/decisions-on-changes-to-the-assessment-of-mathematics-physics-and-combined-science-gcses-in-2025-2026-and-2027).

**Third finding: GCSE calculus routing.** Cambridge 0580 Extended E2.12 allows differentiation of sums of at most three terms with nonnegative integer powers; Pearson 4MA1 Higher 3.4 A–E and modular 4WM2H 3.4 A–E allow elementary polynomial differentiation and simple linear kinematics. **Neither AQA 8300 nor Pearson UK 1MA1** assesses differentiation. Cambridge E2.12 does not require points of inflection. Do not make calculus available simply because a student selects Higher GCSE.

## 2. Verified exact objectives and misconceptions

| Board/route | Objectives | Required learning; potential misconception |
|---|---|---|
| AQA 8300 Foundation | R11 compound measures, R5 ratio, R9 percentages | Convert minutes to hours correctly; compare actual unit cost; percentage reversal is division by multiplier |
| AQA 8300 Higher | G19 similar lengths/areas/volumes; A7 inverse and composite; G10 circle theorems | Square vs cube of scale factor; inverse needs one-to-one domain; circle argument requires theorem |
| Pearson UK 1MA1 Higher | S3 histogram density and cumulative frequency; S6 scatter graphs; G25 vectors | Unequal class width changes density; correlation is not causation; vector proof requires directions |
| Cambridge 0580 Core | C9.3 mean/median/mode/range individual data; C7.1 single transformations; C9.2 compare data | Sort before median; Core C7.1 forbids combination of transformations, negative scale factors; limited sample inference |
| Cambridge 0580 Extended | E2.12 polynomial differentiation; E7.4 vector geometry; E9.3 grouped mean | Derivative powers restricted; collinearity needs scalar multiple; grouped mean is estimated |
| Pearson 4MA1 linear Foundation | 4.4 measures, 6.2 statistical measures, 4.8 A–C 2D right triangle | Squared conversion factor; weighted vs unweighted mean; tangent ratio is not the angle |
| Pearson 4MA1 linear Higher | 1.8 A bounds; 3.4 A–E differentiation/kinematics | Upper bound may be strict; velocity sign distinguishes distance from displacement |
| Pearson 4WM1F / 4WM1H | Unit 1 Foundation 1.1 H factors/HCF, 2.7 A monic quadratics; Unit 1 Higher 2.2 D completing square, 2.7 B quadratic formula | Reject nonphysical roots; do not assign Unit 2-only percentages/functions/calculus to Unit 1 |
| Pearson 4WM2F / 4WM2H | Unit 2 Foundation 1.6 F reverse percentage, 1.7 B ratio; Unit 2 Higher 3.2 B–D functions, 3.4 B–E calculus | Reverse operator, function composition order and inverse domain; cumulative Unit 1 prerequisites |

## 3. Original question blueprint register — **27 editorial designs**, 9 routes × 3 tiers

**Do not copy official exam questions.** The following are independently devised scenarios/parameter seeds for the later authoring routine. They are **not** finished or approved questions. Daily is a 2–5-minute targeted micro-skill; Weekly 6–10-minute multi-step reasoning; Exam is a 9–15-minute integrated problem with method marks, **not a full timed mock**. Final wording, checked private solution, diagram, marking rubric, choice uniqueness, calculator setting and semantic dedup are still required.

| Route | Grove Daily | Grove Weekly | Grove Exam practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AQA Foundation** | **R11:** 72 km in 1 h 36 min; calculate average speed with correct hours. Trap: 1.36 h. | **R5/R11:** compare two packs with different weights/prices for a fixed required total, accounting for whole packs, not just price/kg. | **R11:** minibus 48 km at 60 km/h, waits 15 min, then 30 km at 40 km/h; overall speed includes stop, and explain why arithmetic mean of speeds fails. |
| **AQA Higher** | **G19:** two similar solids, length ratio 2:3; infer volume ratio and explain why not 4:9. | **G19/R12:** similar cylindrical tanks, one volume known; scale to other and evaluate usable fraction, with units. | **A7:** f(x)=3x−4 and g(x)=x²+1 restricted to x≥0; form g∘f with x≥4/3, inverse on appropriate range and test an output. Trap: wrong branch/order. |
| **Pearson UK 1MA1 Higher** | **S3:** histogram bins [0,4) and [4,10) have frequencies 12 and 9; compute frequency densities, not heights as frequencies. | **S6:** create synthetic scatter data with a high-leverage outlier; line of best fit, interpolation and why distant extrapolation is unreliable. | **G25:** parallelogram A(1,1), B(7,3), D(3,5); calculate C and diagonal midpoints, prove they bisect by vectors. |
| **Cambridge 0580 Core** | **C9.3:** six unsorted observations; order, find median and range. | **C7.1:** enlarge triangle A(2,4), B(6,4), C(2,8) from origin by 1/2; coordinates, original and new area; **one** transformation only. | **C9.2/C9.3:** compare two small delivery-time samples with same mean and different ranges; explain consistency and sampling limitation. |
| **Cambridge 0580 Extended** | **E2.12:** differentiate 5x²−4x+3; locate/classify turning point; allowed polynomial powers only. | **E7.4:** A(1,2), B(4,8), C(7,14): show collinearity by vectors, relative position, and test a new off-line point. | **E2.12:** f(t)=t³−6t²+9t+10 on 0≤t≤5; stationary points, local/global extrema and endpoint comparison. |
| **Pearson 4MA1 linear Foundation** | **4.4:** convert an area in km² to m²; require squared conversion. | **6.2:** scores 4,7,10 with frequencies 8,12,5; weighted mean and comparison to another cohort. | **4.8 A–C:** ramp rises 0.9 m over horizontal 3.6 m; length, angle and decision on a 15° maximum, with diagram. |
| **Pearson 4MA1 linear Higher** | **3.4 B–C:** derivative of 4x³−9x²+2 and stationary x-values; avoid solving y=0. | **1.8 A:** rectangle 8.2 cm × 3.6 cm to nearest 0.1; strict bounds on area, justify lower/lower and upper/upper. | **3.4 E:** displacement s(t)=2t³−15t²+36t, 0≤t≤5; stopping instants, displacement versus total distance by sign of velocity. |
| **Pearson modular Unit 1 F/H** | **4WM1F 1.1 H:** maximum equal packs from 84 pencils and 126 erasers, no remainder (HCF). | **4WM1F 2.7 A:** positive integers differing by 5 with product 84; form monic quadratic and reject negative solution. | **4WM1H 2.2 D/2.7 B:** complete square of original non-monic quadratic, find minimum and number of roots at a specified level; no Unit 2 content. |
| **Pearson modular Unit 2 F/H** | **4WM2F 1.6 F:** after 25% discount price is 63; recover original by dividing by 0.75. | **4WM2F 1.7 B:** 80 packets in ratio 2:3:5; move 8 from largest to smallest; new ratio and conservation argument. | **4WM2H 3.2 B–D:** f(x)=2x−1, g(x)=x² restricted to x≥0; compose g∘f on x≥1/2, derive inverse and verify input; Unit 2 only. |

**Misconception codes for feedback:** time-as-decimal, mean-of-speeds, unit-price-whole-packs, linear-vs-cubic-scale, function-order, inverse-domain-branch, histogram-density, correlation-causation, vector-proof, single-vs-combined-transformations, grouped-mean-estimate, squared-unit-conversion, weighted-mean, strict-upper-bound, displacement-vs-distance, reverse-percentage.

**Daily/Weekly/Exam sequencing:** Daily short transferable skill and optional visible working; Weekly 3–5 linked but genuinely different representations, not re-numbered copies; Exam reasoning-rich practice with evidence capture and examiner rubric. Current one-mark MCQ-only marking is not equivalent to multi-mark method scoring.
