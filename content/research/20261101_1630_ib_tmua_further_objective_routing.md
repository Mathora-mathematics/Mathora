## New or clarified syllabus findings

1. **IB first assessment 2029 has downloadable official 2026 subject briefs with usable BROAD topic identifiers, not authorised-guide microcodes.** AA: A1–A5, B1–B5, C1–C3 (C3 HL), D1–D3, E1–E6 (E5–E6 HL); AI: A1–A6 (A6 HL), B1–B5 (B5 HL), C1–C5 (C4–C5 HL), D1–D5, E1–E5 (E5 HL). The AA brief confirms Paper 1 no technology, Paper 2 technology, HL Paper 3 technology. AI requires technology for all papers. Both: 2029 SL 75 marks on each of P1/P2; HL P1/P2 100 marks, P3 50 marks and 60 minutes. The 2029 AA and AI briefs themselves both confirm 60-minute HL Paper 3; earlier public commentary about the *old* duration remains inconsistent with an older subject brief, so configure legacy paper timing from the exam-session guide rather than inferring it from a 2029 comparison.
2. **IB 2029 removals are cohort-specific.** AA SL: financial applications, bivariate data; AA HL also proof by counterexample and Euler's method. AI SL: logarithms and trapezoidal rule; AI HL: complex numbers, vector product, Poisson and certain hypothesis tests. Do not suppress these from valid 2027/28 students; do not route removed objectives to first-assessment-2029 learners. Current guide detailed microcodes remain an authorised-resource access blocker.
3. **TMUA current 2026/27 exact Paper 2 objective coverage extends beyond Arg1–Arg4 and Prf1–Prf2:** the official specification explicitly lists Prf3 conjecture with justification, Prf4 rearranging proof steps, Prf5 sophisticated chains, and Err1–Err2 identifying errors in purported proofs. Paper 1 Section 1; Paper 2 Sections 1 and 2. Both 20 MCQ, 75 min, no calculator or formula booklet, no negative marking. UAT-UK's specimen/practice page says the Pearson player is the official format reference; the public text **does not certify a fixed answer-option count**. Keep authentic TMUA mock status blocked pending desktop player inspection. Its reported 1–9 scaled score cannot be inferred from an uncalibrated private raw score; UAT-UK notes median 4.5 and 90th percentile 7.0 for the score scale.
4. **Cambridge 9231 2028–30 v1 explicitly says no significant changes affecting teaching** relative to existing 2026–27. Keep syllabus-version metadata anyway. AS route: P1+P3 or P1+P4. Full A: P1+P2+P3+P4. P2 is not AS. P2 §2.3 implicit differentiation, §2.4 integration, §2.6 differential equations; P4 §4.3 χ², §4.4 nonparametric, §4.5 PGFs. The 2028–30 official P4 instructions combine classes with expected frequencies below 5; PGFs of independent sums multiply. All components permit scientific calculators and MF19.
5. **Pearson 9FM0** requires both Core Pure papers and a permitted pair of optional papers. FS1 §3.2 geometric expectation/variance, §4.2 geometric testing, §6.1 χ²; Decision 1 §1.3 Eulerian graphs, §2.2 Floyd, §3.1 route inspection. Appendix 9 and Issue 4 correction distinguish 3D Decision 1 from 4D Decision 2. A Cambridge 9231 candidate must not receive Pearson Decision questions by default.
