### Four separately routable papers, not interchangeable with linear 4MA1

| Modular route | Unit entry code | Qualification cash-in | Official exam | Prerequisite/eligibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unit 1 Foundation | 4WM1F/01 | 4XMAF | 120 minutes; 100 raw marks; 50% | Unit 1 Foundation scope only |
| Unit 2 Foundation | 4WM2F/01 | 4XMAF | 120 minutes; 100 raw marks; 50% | Unit 1 Foundation assumed |
| Unit 1 Higher | 4WM1H/01 | 4XMAH | 120 minutes; 100 raw marks; 50% | Unit 1 Foundation plus Unit 1 Higher scope |
| Unit 2 Higher | 4WM2H/01 | 4XMAH | 120 minutes; 100 raw marks; 50% | Unit 1/2 Foundation plus Unit 1 Higher and Unit 2 Higher |

**Do not mix Foundation and Higher tiers between the two modular units** (P1 p.45). Unit 1 and Unit 2 are *not* graded as a deliberately increasing difficulty sequence: both assess the whole grade range of their tier (P1 pp. 1,6–7). Pearson's 4WM1F/4WM1H and 4WM2F/4WM2H codes are **unit entries**, while 4XMAF/4XMAH are **cash-in codes**. Do not infer a student's current unit from a cash-in code alone.

**All four modular exams permit a suitable scientific calculator and provide a tier-specific formula sheet** (P1 pp.6–7, 41–43, 56–57). No CAS/symbolic differentiation, formula retrieval or QWERTY keyboard is permitted in the actual examination. A non-calculator drill may be pedagogically useful but cannot be advertised as authentic 4WM examination conditions. The papers are written, method-marked exams; a single-mark MCQ does not simulate their mark scheme.

**NEW grading/analytics blocker: raw marks are not UMS.** Each written paper has **100 raw marks** but Pearson reports **up to 120 uniform marks (UMS) per unit**, **240 UMS for the full cash-in** (P1 pp.47–48). Fixed published UMS grade thresholds must not be applied to raw percentages or to an arbitrary Grove Exam score. Raw-to-UMS conversion is session-specific; a mock-based predicted grade must state the conversion basis and uncertainty. The unit-re-sit aggregation rule (better of the two most recent attempts) also cannot be approximated by simply taking the lifetime maximum (P1 p.48).

**Assessment balance:** On the *full qualification*, P1 pp.43–44 allocates 57–63% to AO1 number/algebra, 22–28% to AO2 shape/space/measures and 12–18% to AO3 handling data. This is **Pearson International GCSE modular's content-domain AO classification**, not the same labels as the UK GCSE AO1 fluency / AO2 reasoning / AO3 problem solving. Separately, Pearson gives Foundation 60% standard technique, 25% problem solving, 15% reasoning; Higher 50%, 30%, 20%. Mock planning must track both axes without conflating them.

## 2. Exact unit-level learning-objective gates

| Unit | Confirmed specification points | What the student must be able to do | Common false transfer or misconception |
|---|---|---|---|
| **4WM1F** | §3.3 E,G,H; §6.1 A(i); §6.3 C,D,H | Find midpoint and gradient, interpret straight-line/time graphs; interpret two-way tables and probabilities/complements | Averaging coordinates incorrectly; reading a flat distance-time segment as zero distance; double-counting intersection in a table |
| **4WM1H** | §1.4 A–C; §4.8 C–F; §6.1 A; §6.3 C | Manipulate/rationalise surds; solve 3D Pythagoras/trigonometry including angle between line and plane; interpret unequal-width histograms | Treating a 3D diagonal as a face diagonal; using vertical height instead of base projection; using frequency as histogram height |
| **4WM2F** | §5.2 D–I, M; §4.11 A–B; §4.10 E–F | Reflect, rotate, translate and describe transformations; use maps/scale drawings and similar-length ratios; convert prism/cylinder volume | Treating (x,y) reflection in y=x as (-x,-y); confusing length and area scale; forgetting centre, angle or direction |
| **4WM2H** | §4.6 C(i)–(v); §5.1 A–F; §6.1 B–C; §6.2 A–D | Apply circle-angle relationships; add/subtract/scale vectors and find magnitude/resultant; construct/read cumulative frequency, median, IQR | Opposite cyclic angles mistaken as equal; adding vector magnitudes instead of components; reading grouped-data quartiles as exact |

**Unit 1 versus Unit 2 boundary is substantive:** Unit 1 Higher includes surds, conditional probability, histograms and 3D trigonometry; Unit 2 Higher adds inverse/composite functions and differentiation (§3.2/§3.4), circle theorems, vectors and cumulative frequency. Unit 2 Foundation includes transformations and similarity; Unit 1 Foundation does not. The Unit 2 exams can assume Unit 1 prerequisite knowledge, but Unit 1 mock questions must not assess Unit 2-only objectives.

**Cross-board safety:** Pearson linear 4MA1 has two calculator-permitted papers drawing from its full linear scope, whereas modular 4WM restricts content by unit. Pearson UK 1MA1 has a non-calculator paper and no GCSE differentiation; Cambridge 0580 2028–2030 has Core/Extended non-calculator and calculator components with different bounds/differentiation gates. Do not use a generic "IGCSE Higher" flag to override qualification, unit, tier and exam-year.

## 3. Original question blueprints — 12 designs, four routes × three Grove tiers

**These are original editorial seeds, not protected past-paper reproductions, published bank items, finished mark schemes or approved timed mocks.** Mark/time allocations below are design targets for later examiner review. Each seed must become a complete original student prompt, independent full solution, mark-by-mark rubric, misconception-aware feedback, valid KaTeX and diagram where needed. No automatic approval.

### 4WM1F — Foundation Unit 1 (Daily / Weekly / Exam)

**F1-D / Grove Daily (2–3 min, 2 marks):** §3.3 E. A map uses Cartesian coordinates; endpoints of a short path are A(−6,4), B(8,−2). Find the midpoint and explain why each coordinate is averaged separately. **Check:** (1,1). **Trap:** averaging all four numbers into one coordinate or subtracting endpoints. Fingerprint: midpoint / signed coordinates / map segment / two-coordinate output.

**F1-W / Grove Weekly (7–9 min, 6 marks):** §3.3 A,G. Supply an *original* distance–time polyline with points (0 min,0 m), (12,900), (18,900), (30,1500). Ask (a) time spent stationary; (b) first and final moving-stage speeds in m/min; (c) overall mean speed; (d) why the flat segment is not a return journey. **Checks:** stationary 6 min; stage speeds 75 and 50 m/min; overall 50 m/min. **Trap:** using only moving time for whole-journey speed; treating flat graph as zero total distance. Fingerprint: graph-gradient / waiting plateau / three segments / average-speed comparison. Unlike earlier AQA journey wording, this is a graph interpretation task with a flat segment.

**F1-E / Grove Exam reasoning item (10–12 min, 8 marks):** §§6.1 A(i), 6.3 C,D,H. Invent a two-way table of workshop arrival mode and punctuality: bus early 18, bus late 7; cycle early 15, cycle late 10. Require correct margins, P(early), P(bus OR late) *by counting table cells once*, and P(neither bus nor late), with an explanation of the overlap. **Checks:** N=50; early 33/50; union 35/50=7/10; neither 15/50=3/10. **Trap:** adding 25+17 without removing 7 counted twice; do not require general symbolic conditional probability (Higher Unit 1). Fingerprint: two-way-table union / bus-cycle / punctuality / explicit overlap. Distinct from prior 4MA1 Venn diagram seeds.

### 4WM1H — Higher Unit 1

**H1-D / Grove Daily (3–4 min, 3 marks):** §1.4 B. Rationalise 5/(√7+2), keeping an exact answer. **Check:** 5(√7−2)/3. **Trap:** multiplying by √7+2 rather than its conjugate; losing the factor 5. Fingerprint: surd-conjugate / 5-over-root7-plus2.

**H1-W / Grove Weekly (8–11 min, 8 marks):** §4.8 D,F. An original rectangular observatory has floor dimensions 6 m by 8 m and ceiling height 12 m. From one floor corner to the diagonally opposite ceiling corner, find the floor projection, 3D diagonal, and angle between the diagonal and the horizontal floor; explain which triangle contains that angle. **Checks:** floor diagonal 10 m; 3D diagonal √244=2√61≈15.6205 m; angle arctan(12/10)≈50.194°. **Trap:** tan θ=12/8 or 12/6 instead of 12/10; 3D line versus plane angle is measured against its projection. Fingerprint: 3D angle-line-plane / 6-8-12 / observatory.

**H1-E / Grove Exam reasoning item (11–14 min, 9 marks):** §6.1 A. Provide a histogram (or frequency-density table to be plotted) with bins [0,5), [5,15), [15,25) minutes and densities 4, 2.4, 1.6. Ask for class frequencies, sample total, proportion with time below 15 minutes, and whether a claim that the middle bin has the tallest bar because it has the largest frequency is correct. **Checks:** frequencies 20,24,16; total 60; below 15: 44/60=11/15; first bin has greatest *density* and hence tallest bar. **Trap:** treating histogram heights as frequencies with unequal widths. Fingerprint: histogram density / three unequal classes / density4-2.4-1.6 / claim audit. Avoid reusing earlier Pearson UK 1MA1 density-bin data.

### 4WM2F — Foundation Unit 2

**F2-D / Grove Daily (2–3 min, 2 marks):** §5.2 D,E. Reflect P(−3,7) in the line y=x. **Check:** P′(7,−3). **Trap:** sign reversal is not reflection in y=x. Fingerprint: y-equals-x reflection / negative-positive point.

**F2-W / Grove Weekly (8–10 min, 8 marks):** §5.2 A–I,M. Triangle A(1,1), B(3,1), C(1,4) is rotated 90° anticlockwise about (1,−1), then translated by column vector (3,2). Calculate all intermediate and final vertices; compare original and final areas; explain why order of operations matters. **Checks:** rotated A′(−1,−1), B′(−1,1), C′(−4,−1); final A″(2,1), B″(2,3), C″(−1,1); both areas 3 square units. **Trap:** rotation about origin, clockwise rotation, assuming transformation order commutes. Fingerprint: composite rigid transformations / centre(1,-1) / triangle / preserve area. Cambridge Core 0580 transformation restrictions must **not** be inferred for Pearson modular Foundation.

**F2-E / Grove Exam reasoning item (10–13 min, 9 marks):** §4.11 A,B plus Unit 1 area/perimeter prerequisites. A rectangular community site is drawn as 4.8 cm × 3.2 cm on a 1:500 scale plan. Convert to real dimensions, area, total boundary length, then decide how many 25 m fence rolls are needed if a 4 m-wide gate is left unfenced; explain why three rolls are insufficient. **Checks:** 24 m ×16 m; 384 m²; perimeter 80 m; fence 76 m; four whole rolls. **Trap:** multiplying map area by 500 instead of 500², mixing cm/m, rounding 76/25 down. Fingerprint: map scale / rectangular plot / gate / whole-roll procurement.

### 4WM2H — Higher Unit 2

**H2-D / Grove Daily (2–3 min, 2 marks):** §4.6 C(iv). A cyclic quadrilateral has one interior angle 112°. Find the opposite interior angle and state the theorem. **Check:** 68°, opposite angles sum to 180°. **Trap:** using equality rather than supplementary angles. Fingerprint: cyclic opposite angle / 112 degrees.

**H2-W / Grove Weekly (8–10 min, 8 marks):** §5.1 B–F. Three consecutive displacement vectors on a coordinate grid are u=(4,−1), v=(−2,7), w=(5,−3). Find the resultant vector and its magnitude, then the exact fourth displacement needed to return to the start; explain why adding the lengths of the three legs is not the net displacement. **Checks:** resultant (7,3); magnitude √58≈7.6158; return (−7,−3). **Trap:** vector-scalar confusion and wrong signs. Fingerprint: closed-vector-walk / three displacements / resultant / return. Avoid the earlier Pearson UK parallelogram diagonal proof construction.

**H2-E / Grove Exam reasoning item (12–15 min, 10 marks):** §§6.1 B,C; 6.2 A,D. A synthetic grouped waiting-time table uses intervals 0–10, 10–20, 20–30, 30–40 minutes with frequencies 8,12,20,10 (N=50). Construct cumulative frequencies at upper boundaries; interpolate an estimated median and quartiles; obtain estimated IQR; estimate fraction at most 30 minutes and explain the interpolation assumption. **Checks:** cumulative 8,20,40,50; median≈22.5 min, Q1≈13.75 min, Q3≈28.75 min, IQR≈15 min; at most 30: 40/50=0.8. **Trap:** treating grouped estimates as exact observations; wrong quartile positions; interpolation without using class widths. Fingerprint: cumulative-frequency interpolation / 8-12-20-10 / wait times / IQR.

**Authoring checks completed at blueprint level:** the geometry, vector, table-count, histogram, map and cumulative-frequency numerical outputs above were independently recalculated. These are not a substitute for testing finished student wording and full mark schemes. Item-level similarity checks must be rerun on the live bank just before publication.
