Examiner's overview

Alex demonstrates confident algebraic manipulation and generally accurate numerical work. Marks are most often lost when a multi-step problem requires an explanation before calculation, or when probability events are not clearly identified.

What is working well

  • Clear expansion and factorisation with methods visible.
  • Reliable handling of fractions and negative values.
  • Good correction habits when checking a result.

Where marks were lost

  • Probability: incomplete sample spaces and unclear event descriptions.
  • Geometry: using a numerical answer without naming the relevant theorem.
  • Longer questions: occasional omissions in written reasoning.

EXAMINER'S NEXT STEPS

  1. Days 1–3: complete two short sample-space sets with explicit event definitions.
  2. Days 4–7: practise geometry explanations, identifying the theorem before the calculation.
  3. Week 2: complete a mixed review and compare the underlying methods.
  4. Next review: check whether previous misconceptions have recurred.
Indicative grade caution

A grade estimate requires appropriate specification coverage, calibrated boundaries and sufficient exam evidence. This specimen deliberately does not claim a validated predicted grade.
