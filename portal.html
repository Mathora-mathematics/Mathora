BEFORE YOU START

Exam conditions, thoughtfully designed.

Only Grove Exam members can start a timed mock. Read the paper's duration and calculator instructions, have your writing materials ready, and keep your device connected to upload working. You may type, draw on squared paper, or attach a photograph to each question. Your examiner reviews submitted evidence after the sitting.

Opening your available mock examinations…

A full mock appears only after a teacher has authored, checked, approved and assigned its complete paper. Our original pilot practice sets are not full examination mocks.
